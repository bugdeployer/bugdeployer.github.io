Building Multiplexr: collections, spaces and playback.

A look at the Android media app I’m developing: what works so far and what’s next.

Multiplexr is an Android media app I’m developing for local movies, TV, videos and audiobooks. It hasn’t been released. These are the features in the current development build, with testing still underway.

I started it for my own library. I wanted folder-based collections, artwork and playback history in one place.

Collections

A collection points to one or more root folders. Scanning builds an index without moving the original files.

The development build supports Video → Movies & TV, Video → Videos, and Audio → Audiobooks. Collections have search, sorting, filters, tags and browsing layouts. You can choose the built-in player or an external player, and whether a title opens its details or starts playback. Series open their contents first.

Spaces

Cinema, Videos and Audiobooks have dedicated browsing spaces. Each space can show a single collection or combine eligible collections. A collection excluded from the global space still has its own local space.

Space settings include showcase artwork, up to three ordered showcase titles, and browsing preferences. Continue watching and Continue listening use the same saved progress as the collection views.

Video and audiobook playback

The video player has resume, subtitles, audio tracks, playback speed and queues. TV series use seasons and episodes.

Audiobooks use series → audiobook → chapters. A standalone audiobook has a one-title series. An audiobook can be one M4B or several audio files; chapters are handled inside the player. The current controls include chapter navigation, bookmarks, speed, sleep timers and background playback.

I’m still checking playback interruptions, process restarts and device-specific background behavior. Having a control implemented doesn’t mean it’s finished testing.

Metadata and artwork

Movie and TV lookup uses TMDB. Audiobook lookup supports Audible and AudiobookDB. Required provider credentials are entered in Settings → Metadata.

Search results are paginated. Before applying a match, you can compare the current information with the result and choose which fields to replace. Bulk jobs support review, retry and cancellation.

Artwork can come from online lookup or a device image. An audiobook series can also use a cover from one of its members. Choosing a series cover leaves the individual covers alone.

Themes and saved data

Retro and Modern are the bundled styles. Theme work now includes component styling, fonts, icons and portable theme packages, alongside background and accent colors.

App and collection locks use device authentication. They control access inside Multiplexr; they don’t encrypt source files. Library backups contain organization and playback state, not media or provider credentials.

What’s next

The current focus is testing the shared collection, metadata, playback and backup workflows with larger libraries and physical devices. I’m also checking that future media types can reuse those services without each needing its own implementation.

Music, photos, ebooks and comics are planned. They aren’t available in the app yet.

I’ll post progress on the blog and Twitter / X.
